FICTIONAL DEMO

Guard attendance to payroll, end to end

An invented guard travels to an invented post on a hand-drawn map. Inside the post’s radius the phone unlocks TIME IN, a simulated face check runs, and the attendance row reaches the web console as it happens. The admin then reviews attendance, runs payroll for the cut-off from the logs and issues a payslip the guard can open on the phone.
