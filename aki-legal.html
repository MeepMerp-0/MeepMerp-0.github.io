Privacy Notice

1. Who we are

Aki is operated by Jason Selerio, an individual based in Abu Dhabi, United Arab Emirates, who decides how the personal information described here is used (the "controller"). Contact: jason.selerio@gmail.com.

2. Aki is AI

Aki is an automated AI assistant. It is not Jason and does not speak for Jason or any employer.

3. What we process

Your message; up to 6 earlier messages your browser sends with each turn, with a random conversation ID and the signatures and times attached to Aki's earlier replies; technical data: your IP address (used only to estimate your country and to make a keyed hash for rate limits; never stored in raw form or shared with AI providers), your country, and a temporary session ID; and a Cloudflare Turnstile bot check. Before you chat, you confirm you are 18 or older and choose whether to allow Gemini. When a chat session starts we keep a consent record: session ID, notice version, your 18+ confirmation, whether you allowed Gemini, the time and your country.

4. Why, and on what basis

  • Answering your questions about Jason's work: our legitimate interest in running the portfolio, and the steps you ask for.
  • Security, abuse prevention and rate limiting: legitimate interest.
  • Keeping your chat in your browser (section 10): legitimate interest in letting you pick up where you left off. It stays on your device, and you can delete it anytime with Clear.
  • The Gemini option: a box that is pre-ticked on Aki's welcome screen where Gemini is available. You can untick it before continuing, or change it later via "Consent settings". We do not treat a pre-ticked box as a legal basis.
  • Consent and age records: legitimate interest in showing the choices you made.

Where a law, such as the UAE's data protection law, relies on different bases, we rely on the equivalent basis it provides.

5. Who receives what

  • GitHub (site hosting): This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs visitors' IP addresses, whether or not they are signed in to GitHub.
  • Cloudflare: runs the Aki service and the Turnstile bot check. It receives your IP address and the content of your requests in order to deliver them, and Turnstile collects browser and device signals to tell people from bots.
  • Cloudflare Workers AI (backup): if Groq is busy, has used its daily share or fails to answer, Aki may ask a Meta Llama model run by Cloudflare's Workers AI instead. It receives your message, recent conversation and Aki's instructions, the same as Groq, but not your IP address, session ID or country from us. Cloudflare states that it does not use Workers AI inputs or outputs to train models.
  • Groq: answers when you haven't allowed Gemini, where Gemini isn't offered, and as a backup when Gemini fails to answer (not after a Gemini safety decline or a cancel). It receives your message, recent conversation and Aki's instructions. It does not receive your IP address, session ID or country. Groq does not train on this data. Groq may keep logs up to 30 days for reliability and abuse. Processed in the United States.
  • Google Gemini, first choice for adults where available: if you are in an eligible region and leave the Gemini box ticked, Gemini answers first. The box is ticked by default where Gemini is available; untick it before continuing if you don't want your messages sent to Google, and Aki will use Groq instead. If Gemini fails to answer, the same message and conversation are sent to Groq instead, so one message can reach both. This does not happen if Gemini declines a message on safety grounds (nothing is re-sent to Groq) or if you cancel or disconnect while Gemini is working (nothing is sent to Groq). When Gemini answers, Google receives your message as you typed it, your recent conversation with Aki, and Aki's instructions. When Gemini's daily limit is reached or it is paused, Google may answer with a Gemma model through the same Gemini API, under the same terms and with the same data. Earlier messages that contain Jason's contact details, or anything that looks like an email address or phone number, are left out. It does not receive your IP address, session ID or country from us. On Gemini's free tier Google is not our processor: it uses this data for its own purposes, as an independent controller, under its own privacy policy (Google Privacy Policy).
  • We do not sell your information or share it for advertising.

6. What Google does with Gemini data

On the free tier, Google may use what you send, and Gemini's answers, to provide, improve and develop its products, and human reviewers may read, annotate and process them. Google keeps prompts and responses for 55 days to detect and prevent violations of its policies. For improving its products it may keep them longer, and it does not publish how long. Do not send personal or sensitive information.

Sources: Gemini API usage policies, last updated 2026-06-09, and the Gemini API Additional Terms.

7. Choosing Gemini, and changing your mind

Gemini is optional. The box is ticked by default where Gemini is available; untick it before continuing if you don't want your messages sent to Google, and Aki will use Groq instead. It is a pre-ticked choice, and you can untick it before continuing or change it anytime via Consent settings. With the box unticked, Groq answers and nothing goes to Google. To change your choice, use "Consent settings" in the chat; it applies from your next message. Anything already sent to Google can't be recalled.

8. Regional availability

  • Gemini is never used in the EEA, UK or Switzerland, in regions where Gemini is unavailable, or when we can't determine your location; Groq answers instead.
  • In some regions, including comprehensively sanctioned countries, and over Tor, Aki gives only its fixed quick answers and uses no AI provider.
  • Location is estimated from your IP address and can be wrong.

9. Age

Aki is for people 18 or older. Before chatting you confirm that you are; this is a self-declaration and we don't verify it. If you're under 18, please choose "Not now". Viewing the portfolio doesn't require age confirmation.

10. Conversation memory

  • Your conversation is stored only in your browser. Our servers don't keep it.
  • Outside the EEA, UK and Switzerland, your conversation is kept in your browser automatically for 7 days, then older messages are removed.
  • In the EEA, UK and Switzerland, or if we can't tell where you are, it is kept only until you close the tab.
  • There is no Remember setting. Use "Clear", or clear this site's data in your browser, to delete it anytime.
  • Aki's replies carry a signature and a time so Aki can check that earlier replies sent back with a new question are genuine. Replies older than 24 hours are still shown to you but are no longer used as context.

11. Retention

  • Aki's servers keep no message content.
  • Rate-limit counters (keyed hashes) and turn records are deleted within 48 hours.
  • Consent records (one per chat session) are deleted within 25 hours.
  • The session token stays in your browser for up to 24 hours or until the tab closes.
  • Provider retention is as stated above.

12. International transfers

  • Aki's operator is in the UAE, Cloudflare runs on a global network, and Groq processes in the United States. Transfers to Cloudflare and Groq rely on the providers' contractual safeguards (for example Groq's data processing terms and standard contractual clauses, where applicable).
  • If you allow Gemini, your message goes to Google (which may process it in the United States and elsewhere) according to the Gemini choice shown for that chat session (pre-ticked unless you unticked it); no data processing agreement covers it.

13. Children

Aki is for adults (18+) and is not directed to children. Please don't share personal information. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we will delete what we hold. We cannot recall anything already sent to Google through the Gemini option.

14. Your rights, wherever you are

  • You can ask to access, correct or delete your information, object to or restrict its use, withdraw consent, and, where applicable, receive a copy in a portable form.
  • Most of it exists only in your browser, where you control it directly. On our side we hold short-lived keyed counters and turn records, which we cannot link to you without your IP address. We hold a consent record for each chat session for up to 25 hours. For what Google holds after a Gemini turn, contact Google under its privacy policy.
  • Contact jason.selerio@gmail.com. We will answer within the time your local law requires, and within one month at most.
  • You can complain to the data protection authority where you live or work (for example in the EU/EEA, the UK, the UAE or the Philippines).

15. Contact form

If you use the contact form on this site instead of Aki, your name, email address and message are processed by the site's contact service, which emails them to Jason and may record them in a Google Sheets spreadsheet through a Google Apps Script. That is separate from Aki.

16. Limits of AI

Aki's answers are generated by AI and can be wrong. See the Terms of Use.
