# 01: Strategy and content

## Purpose

Turn the confirmed portfolio direction and permitted evidence into one run-specific creative brief. Keep proposals distinguishable from confirmed facts. The brief guides specification; it does not authorize implementation or publication.

## Process

1. Choose the run ID and read only the listed sections. Record the source revisions and relevant human edits using the [run contract](../../shared/run-contract.md#artifact-metadata); for a public demo artifact, use public revisions and sanitized evidence IDs only.
2. Frame the primary goal as mid-level full-time interviews within 90 days: UAE onsite/hybrid and international remote. Keep freelance partnerships and brand as secondary outcomes. Map Hero, Work, About, Process, Contact to visitor questions, public evidence, and actions; use primary navigation labels Work, About, Process, Contact.
3. Record Premium Cinematic option D in light and dark themes, including the confirmed lightweight real Three.js Hero, separate from Aki's original robot and the pinned Work showcase. In Work, use the confirmed scene sequence Aki, INNERCIRCLE, Payroll/Attendance, FuturoHub. Treat any remaining content order as proposed. Outline immediate readable Hero text/actions, ordinary Hero scroll into Work, native page-scroll Work pinning, mobile and reduced-motion alternatives, and loading/failure fallback for specification. Apply the [adviser guidance](../../shared/model-routing.md#adviser) to motion tradeoffs.
4. Present Aki as the existing live, functional assistant. Its original robot assets, geometry, proportions, materials, colors, animation, launcher appearance, placement, and behavior are protected; only the chat panel presentation may be refined within the existing functional contracts. Place INNERCIRCLE only at its existing public summary ceiling. Flag any unverified claim as an open decision.
5. Present the brief, claim limits, and meaningful design tradeoffs at a creative checkpoint. Incorporate steering within existing authority; record unresolved decisions rather than inventing facts.
6. Run the audit below before saving the selected run's brief from the [template](references/artifact-template.md). A blocked or draft brief may state unmet criteria, but cannot claim completion.

For a demo run, choose only a permitted independent fictional concept and record approved public facts, invented behavior, and proposed interaction separately. Use sanitized public evidence IDs and public revision hashes; keep exact private locations, revisions, inspection notes, and scan results outside the public checkout and its outputs. Payroll/Attendance must be synthetic and non-statutory; FuturoHub must use fresh fictional copy. INNERCIRCLE defaults to an abstract scene within its existing high-level public summary, without reproduced internal UI, architecture, or claims. Do not propose a canned, mock, or duplicate Aki walkthrough. Make each fictional concept visibly local, in-memory, resettable, and no-real-action. Apply the selected [catalog boundaries](../../shared/demo-catalog.md#payroll-and-attendance) only within these limits.

## Checkpoints

- **Creative:** Show the proposed hierarchy, key scenes, recruiter path, Aki entry point, mobile/reduced-motion approach, and claims requiring evidence. Apply [checkpoint authority](../../shared/run-contract.md#checkpoints-and-authority).

## Audit

- **Pass** if every substantive claim maps to the proof register or is labeled a proposal; **fail** for invented outcomes, metrics, or confidential INNERCIRCLE detail.
- **Pass** if the hiring path and secondary audiences are clear and Aki remains discoverable; **fail** if either disappears in the visual concept.
- **Pass** if the chosen direction and accessible alternatives can guide a spec; **fail** if the brief relies on effects without content purpose or leaves open choices disguised as decisions.
- **Pass** if source revisions, human edits, and status follow the run contract; **fail** if a stale draft is presented as current.
- **Demo runs:** Pass if the brief identifies capability, alias, approved public facts, fictional scope, interaction, and sanitized public evidence; fail for private provenance in a public artifact or a claim beyond its disclosure ceiling.
