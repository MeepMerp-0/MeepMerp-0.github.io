# Portfolio strategy

## Confirmed goals

The primary outcome for the next 90 days is **interviews for a full-time mid-level engineer role**. Secondary outcomes are freelance or consulting inquiries, partnerships, and personal brand. This priority concerns the portfolio's message and calls to action; it does not assert that an interview or engagement has occurred.

## Hiring target

The target arrangement is UAE on-site or hybrid and international remote. Use **mid-level** for the desired seniority. Full-stack apps/MVPs, AI agents/automation, business systems/dashboards/APIs, and technical leadership/architecture are capabilities to show through evidence, not claims to senior title or years of authority. Current role names and employment dates remain factual source records; a target role does not rewrite them.

## Audiences and markets

Recruiters and technical hiring managers need a rapid view of role fit, scope, credible work, and how to contact Jason. Startup founders, business owners and operations teams, and agencies need a clear picture of problems he can solve and the delivery evidence. Target markets are the UAE, wider GCC/Middle East, Philippines/SE Asia, and global remote opportunities. No relative market ranking was confirmed.

## Positioning

Lead with credible mid-level engineering fit and practical full-stack and AI/business-systems work. Make project summaries scannable for both technical and nontechnical visitors. Emphasize INNERCIRCLE and Aki as proof within their respective disclosure limits. Treat portfolio project technology as evidence of past work, not automatic dependencies for this site's implementation. Any new headline, availability wording, employment claim, or project metric must be checked against [public proof rules](proof-register.md#evidence-rules) and the active [content records](repo-map.md#application-map).

The confirmed page order is **Hero → Work → About → Process → Contact**, with Work/About/Process/Contact navigation. Work presents **Aki → INNERCIRCLE → Timekeeping, Payroll & Attendance → FuturoHub**. The [catalog](demo-catalog.md#presentation) gives the approved visual scene concepts; exact geometry, camera, timing, and local interaction details remain specification choices. Keep the primary recruiter path concise and the secondary client/partner paths visible. Fictional concepts cannot establish production performance, compliance, or outcomes.

## Success measures

Use interview conversations as the primary outcome signal over the 90-day window. Useful supporting signals include qualified recruiter/hiring-manager contacts, relevant freelance or partnership inquiries, and whether visitors can find and understand the two lead examples and a contact route. Baselines, numeric targets, attribution, and tracking methods are open; do not invent them. A spec may propose measurable usability and performance acceptance criteria for approval.
