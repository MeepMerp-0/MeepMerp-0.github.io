**Mobile/attendance concept (`payroll-mobile`).** Show a fictional guard approaching a drawn proximity zone, tapping an illustrated phone, passing a stylized image/biometric-check *visual step*, then automatically adding a synthetic row to a local web dashboard. Animate or step through this causal sequence; reduced motion presents the same steps in order. No actual GPS/geofence, camera, face image or biometric analysis, authentication, device permission, attendance submission, network, storage, or background synchronization. Visual depiction of a check is permitted; sensing or identifying a person is not.
